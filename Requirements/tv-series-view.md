# Epic: TV Series View

The detail screen for a TV series, reached by tapping a TV series from a person's page. All new work is SwiftUI.

| # | Story | Done |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | If you click on a TV series from a person's page, you are pushed to this view. It shows the poster, TV series name, first air date and last aired date, creator, and genres. | [ ] |
| 2 | Underneath that, a carousel of images for the TV series. Clicking on an image makes it full screen (and can be dismissed). | [ ] |
| 3 | Underneath the above, a carousel of seasons. Each cell contains: name, air date, number of episodes, and profile image. Sorted by season number (earliest first). | [ ] |
| 4 | Underneath the above, a carousel of recommendations. Each cell contains: poster and TV show name. Clicking a TV show pushes you to that TV show. | [ ] |
| 5 | Underneath the above, a carousel of actors in the series. Sort by episode count (highest first). Limit to 30 people. UI includes a picture, the character name, and the actor's real name. Clicking a cell takes you to that person. | [ ] |
| 6 | Underneath the above, a carousel of directors and writers. Sort by episode count (highest first). Limit to 30 people. UI includes a picture, the character name, and the actor's real name. Clicking a cell takes you to that person. | [ ] |
| 7 | Underneath that, a list of reviews. This can paginate, so handle pagination. Each cell includes: the reviewer's content, the reviewer's name, their username, and the last updated date of the review. | [ ] |
