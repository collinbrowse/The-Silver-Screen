# Epic: Favorites & Bookmarking

Let users bookmark movies (and eventually TV series and people) and browse them in a dedicated Favorites tab. All new work is SwiftUI.

> **Required work:** Stories 1 and 2 are **required** and must be completed after the [Top Movies](top-movies.md) epic. Everything else in this epic (and the rest of the backlog) is optional — pick what you want.
